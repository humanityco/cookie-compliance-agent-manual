Shared layout

Cookie categories

Four groups a banner can list

Necessary

Cookies the shop uses to load pages, keep a cart, and store the consent choice.

Preferences

Cookies that remember choices such as language or a saved list.

Statistics

Cookies that count visits and show which pages people open.

Marketing

Cookies that measure ads and remember a campaign someone arrived from.

These names illustrate the groups a banner can list on a page like this.
